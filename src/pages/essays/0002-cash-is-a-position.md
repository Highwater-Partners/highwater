title: Cash Is a Position
dek: Holding cash is a decision with a thesis behind it, not a failure to act.

The reflex to be fully invested treats cash as an embarrassment — capital that
has not yet found a home. That gets it backwards. Cash is a position with its
own thesis: that the opportunity set on offer today is worse than the
opportunity set patience will produce.

Held that way, cash does two things at once. It is dry powder for the moment the
crowd is forced to sell. And it is a standing statement that you would rather
own nothing than own something you cannot defend. Both are underrated.

The discipline is to hold cash *on purpose* and be able to say why — not to
drift into it because you are afraid, and not out of it because you are bored.
